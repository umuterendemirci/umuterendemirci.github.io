Hybrid-casual tower defense · Unity 6 · Android and browser

Bonemarch

Draft your towers. Hold the gate.

A calm 3D tower defense for phones. Before each level you draft your defence, two cards from three, and build on pads beside the road; you start each wave when you're ready, and draft again after every wave. Each level is a new place on an island, with one landmark that changes where towers belong (a bridge, a hill, a fen, a fork in the road, ruins) and one new skeleton that asks a new question.

I designed the rules, the draft, the five towers, seven enemies and ten boons, the ember economy, the landmarks and the brief for every level, and set the difficulty targets. Bots playing the game's own rules fitted each level to those targets. The eight levels in this build are AI-assisted drafts I am redesigning.

How it's designed

Each part below condenses one chapter of the game design document. Every summary ends with a link to the full chapter in the PDF.

01The game at a glance

Draft, build, defend, draft again. A level runs 2.5 to 13.5 minutes of fighting at the player's pace: nothing moves until Start, and 2× speed is optional.

  • Calm, not slow: difficulty comes from new enemies and the map, not from rushing.
  • The draft is the strategy: every run of a level is a different hand.
  • Every level is a place: its landmark moves the towers.
Read the full chapter in the GDD (page 1)

02One level, step by step

Read the map, draft two cards, build on glowing pads, start the wave. Kills drop embers for upgrades, crystals appear around the map, and each wave clear brings one more pick, with boons from level 3. An Ember Surge softens the first wave of each new enemy. Stars come from lives left.

Read the full chapter in the GDD (page 2)

03Seven enemies, ten boons

Shieldbearers shrug off weak hits, Runners rush, Brutes fall apart, Wraiths fly over Cannon and Flame, Necromancers heal, and Bone Giants cost five lives at the gate. Every tower is worth 1 to 10 points of win rate to the bots; boons barely move it, because a boon replaces a tower card.

Read the full chapter in the GDD (page 3)

04Eight places, one new idea each

Each level is written in a plain text file the game, the tests and the bots all read, with a checker that names the line of any mistake. Maps grow from 9 × 13 to 17 × 22 tiles. The drafts were made with an AI assistant from my brief so the landmark rules could be built and tested; I am redrawing them.

Read the full chapter in the GDD (page 4)

05Tuned by bots, read by a designer

Careless, first-time and practised bots play every level 300 times. Their results set each level's health, showed that the generated late levels ran 24 and 37 minutes, and that after the second pass the right counter is worth 11 to 21 points on levels 3 to 5. Bonemarch has not been playtested by people yet.

Read the full chapter in the GDD (page 5)

06Art, audio, tech and the roadmap

Low-poly 3D on free CC0 kits, effects at every target, two music loops that cross-fade at the same bar. The rules live in a pure C# assembly with 71 tests, shared by the game and the bots. A monetisation plan (not built) and a roadmap close the document.

Read the full chapter in the GDD (page 6)
